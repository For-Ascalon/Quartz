### Notes

These mighty warriors are the elite infantry of the Red Hand Horde. They charge into battle, clashing their battleaxes together, challenging anyone to face them.  Use the [orog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17189-orog) stat block, but give them AC 13 (hide armor) with 51 (6d8+24) hit points and a pair of battleaxes instead of a greataxe, for 2x 8 (1d8+4) damage each round.

These warriors often ride the draconic "hellhounds", or worgs to reach the battlefield, but turn them loose to better fight on solid ground.
