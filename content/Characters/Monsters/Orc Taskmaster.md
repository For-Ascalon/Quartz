### Notes

Replacing the RHoD "Hobgoblin Sergeant" - this uses the [bugbear](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16817-bugbear) stat block, but gives them a 'chain shirt' for AC 17, changes the Morningstar to a Whip, and lets them make two attacks: +4 to hit, 7 (2d4+2) slashing damage (~14 per round).
