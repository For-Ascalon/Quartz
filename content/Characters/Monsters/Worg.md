### Notes

Wicked, monstrous creatures like Chimera, Manticores, and Worgs emerged during the ancient past, when supernatural forces roamed freely through the world.  Worgs are often mistaken for dire wolves, but they are not the loping, lithe pack hunters the Orcs tamed long ago; rather, they are sapient, cruel hunters who taunt their prey in their own twisted language before ripping them apart in an ecstatic frenzy.

Some scholars claim their origins lie in the Winter Court of the Unseelie Fey, where Oberon bred them to compete with the deadly malks of Queen Mab during Wild Hunts. Where malks are elegant masters of silently stalking prey and springing on them from the shadows, worgs delight in roaring, howling bloodlust and raw strength.

This might be true, but if so worgs have lived in the mortal world for so long they have evolved away from their fey roots and become monstrosities.
