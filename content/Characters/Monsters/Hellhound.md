### Notes

For this campaign we're using the [Dire Wolf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775812-dire-wolf) stat block, with the addition of a weak breath weapon, to align with appropriate CR:

- **Recharge 5-6**: 15ft cone, 9 (2d6+2) fire damage, DC 13 Dexterity save for half damage.

Flavor them as demonic, hybrid abominations between a wolf and a dragon, as opposed to "hellish".
