### Notes
This uses the [Half Dragon](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195068-half-dragon) stat block, with Acid as the elemental type. It can't fly, but it can leap up to 30ft for 10ft of movement as a bonus action.

These unholy abominations are the result of profane rituals that conjure demonic spirits into unhatched dragon eggs.  After a brief gestation, a half-dragon fiend emerges and rapidly grows to its full size (7-8ft high - ignore the art)
