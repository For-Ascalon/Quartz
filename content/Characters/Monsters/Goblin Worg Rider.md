### Notes

These are tougher than regular goblins, being born into the violent tribes of the Cinderspires. They use the CR 1 [Goblin Boss](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195047-goblin-boss) stat block, with a Spear instead of a Scimitar. As cavalry scouts, they also have [Mounted Combatant](https://www.dndbeyond.com/feats/36-mounted-combatant)

- **Melee Advantage** against unmounted foes smaller than your mount.
- Can force attacks to target them instead of mount.
- Mount has Evasion (DEX saves: success = no damage; failure = 1/2 damage)
