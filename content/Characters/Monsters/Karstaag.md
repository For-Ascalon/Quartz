### Notes
Karstaag is an old giant living in self-imposed exile in the Witchwood. He haunts the ruins of his tribe's old steading, mourning its loss hundreds of years after a small army from Vraath Keep razed it, driving his tribe out into the Cinderspire mountains.

He uses the [Hill Giant](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16923-hill-giant) stat block, with the **Fling** action:

_**Fling**_. The giant tries to throw a Small or Medium creature within 10 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or be hurled up to 60 feet horizontally in a direction of the giant’s choice and land [prone](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#ProneCondition), taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet it was thrown.
