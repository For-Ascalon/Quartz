### Notes
The hobgoblins of the original campaign and the standard [Orc](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16972-orc) stat block are both 1/2 in 5e, but the orc has much lower armor, more health, and higher damage (a little).  Replace the Greataxe with a Battleaxe for 8 (1d10+3) damage instead of 9.

You can also replace the Javelin for a Longbow, for +3 to hit, 5 (1d8+1) piercing damage.
