# Description

Jarla is a fair-skinned woman from the frigid lands of Hyperborea who arrived several years ago and bought out the previous general store owner. She buys finished goods like clothes, tableware, common supplies, and finer things like wine and glassware shipped from Denovir.

#### SECRET:

Running the store allows her to observe travelers and report juicy marks to the local bandit gangs, who know her as "the Red Woman" due to her skill, and enthusiasm, when wielding her daggers. She is responsible for a handful of unsolved murders since her arrival. She sees the approach of the Horde as an opportunity to pillage and murder to her heart's content, then disappear in the chaos.
