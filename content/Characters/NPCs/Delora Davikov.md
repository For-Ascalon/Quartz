# Description
A no-nonsense woman, formerly a member of the famed Goldenshield Company, Delora is tall and broad-shouldered, with blond-grey hair gathered in a single braid. Friends with [[Captain Sorana]] and trusted by the town council for wise advice.

Since her retirement 20 years ago, Delora runs the livery stable in Davik's Ferry. She stables mounts for a modest fee, and buys/sells as opportunities present themselves. Merchants passing through rely on her services.
