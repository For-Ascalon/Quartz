#### What Koth Knows
He's hostile, so magic is necessary to get information from him. Charm, detect thoughts, suggestion, etc.  He's a warlord, but relatively new and not fully trusted.
- The Horde gathers at Cinder Hill, under Warlord Kharn's command.
- Four Warlords lead the Horde - Koth, Saarvith (goblin), Ulwai (orc), and Kharn (orc).
	- Saarvith was sent to the ruins of Corvo on a secret mission, and Kharn leads the Horde. Not sure what Ulwai is doing.
- Azar Kul is War Chief, they call him the Dragon-born. Blessed by the Scaled Tyrant, he remains in the Black Temple - Koth doesn't know where that is.
- The Horde is many thousand strong - all the mountain tribes, even goblins, plus several dragons. Koth has only interacted with Ozyrrandion, Fang of the Forest.
- Skull Creek bridge is critical to the Horde's march down from the mountains - the Old Road is too steep and treacherous. It would delay plans by several days if they had to trek through the swamps.
- Davik's Ferry is first to fall - a proving ground to show the Vale folk that resistance is futile. Koth is to lead the attack himself; Ozyrrandion or some ambitious chieftan could take his place.

This info can also be gleaned from materials in the war room at Vraath Keep: notes, reports, maps, etc. Some is coded - make an Intelligence check, and give one piece of non-obvious info for every 5 points.
