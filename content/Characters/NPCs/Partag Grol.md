# Description
Partag Grol (*Wrestles Boar*) is an orc who left the vast eastern plains of Sarmatia seven years ago and crossed the Carpathians to find a new home. He's never opened up about why he left, but despite the tepid welcome he received, most villagers have come to respect his beer if not his heritage.

Partag knows much about the orc clans of Sarmatia, and a bit about the threat coming down from the Cinderspires. He walks with a slight limp - which he'll tell you was a broken leg that healed poorly - but it only bothers him when the weather gets cold.
