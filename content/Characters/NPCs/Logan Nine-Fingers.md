#### Description
Logan is a heavily-scarred man with a bent nose, uneven features, missing both a piece of an ear and the middle finger of his left hand. 

Use the [Berserker (CR2)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4904621-berserker) but amp it up to CR 3 with more HP, bonus action dagger attack, a scimitar multi-attack, and regenerate 10hp/round unless damaged by silver.

When **bloodied** he transforms into a hybrid wolf-man form and attacks the nearest creature until knocked unconscious, he regenerates above bloodied, or nobody remains to be fought, in which case he drinks himself unconscious. In this hybrid state he also has a Bite attack.
