# Description

A grim man in his prime with an unruly mane of tawny hair that frames his scarred and careworn face. He looks at you with stern, grey eyes - there is no laughter hiding there, only cold, calculating judgement. This is clearly the Lion of Brindol.

## Appearance

- **Height & Build**: ~6ft, heavily muscled, clearly a fighter
- **Apparent Age**: late 40s, but weather worn and terribly scarred right-side of face
- **Hair & Eyes**: long, tawny hair hangs loose around shoulders, eyes grey and cold

## Equipment

- +2 mithral battleaxe, restored +1 Lyran shortsword, 25sp, 20gp, four rubies worth 25gp each

* full plate armor with a bronze lion face on chest piece and pauldrons
* thick belt with leather pouch containing a _Potion of Healing (Superior)_

## Motivation

- **Desires**: peace, safety for his people, justice, rebuild Dacia
- **Fears**: dragons, black magic, helplessness
