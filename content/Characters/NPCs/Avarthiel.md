# Description

A solemn, elven woman of great beauty, whose duty is to monitor the interaction between civilization and the wilds, ensuring the Witchwood is treated with the proper respect. She prefers to use persuasion and reason to encourage proper stewardship, but has been known to fly into a fierce rage, startling those who don't expect her to Wildshape into a slavering wolf.

Rarely seen without her gnarled ivy staff, topped by a large tourmaline gem that compliments her pale auburn hair, or her faithful animal companion Fezzik, the ferret.

### The Ghostlord

Avarthiel becomes much more interesting if she isn't simply "afraid of a lich."

Instead, she grew up with the stories every elven druid hears.

Perhaps she was taught:

- "Never travel west of the stone lions."
- "Do not disturb the Lion of Ash."
- "He remembers names older than kingdoms."

To her, the Ghostlord isn't a monster from a fairy tale. He's an almost biblical figure.
A cautionary tale. A druid who should have crossed the Veil centuries ago.
