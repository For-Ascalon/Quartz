# Description
 A selfish misanthrope whose sour manner and legendary stubbornness have caused no end of trouble over the years. Miserly and cruel, his two large, ill-tempered dogs terrorize village children. Argues against evacuation because he can't stand the thought of the money he'd lose if the town is sacked.

## Services
* Runs a warehouse for storing goods for traveling merchants as they wait out bad weather or other favorable travel conditions heading west.
