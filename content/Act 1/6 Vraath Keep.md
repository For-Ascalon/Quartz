**Timeline**: the Horde reaches the keep on day 11, if not delayed.

_Looming out of the shadowy woods ahead is a haunting sight—a ruined keep. The old castle sits on a small rocky hillock, and you can catch glimpses of a broken tower between the trees. A moss-covered stone at the side of the road you’re following marks a footpath that looks like it leads up to the keep._

- 20 minute scramble up the hill to avoid the path
- 5 minute hike on path (DC 10 Survival: many humanoid & several large wolf tracks, recent)

_The old keep is in very poor repair. The gatehouse is partially collapsed, as is a section of wall to the south. A small wooden building sits next to the remains of a long-abandoned garden in front of the structure. The walls surrounding the keep are about fifteen feet high, with a two-story tower looming in the southwest corner of the courtyard within. Large boulders lie strewn amid the ruins of the two watch towers, and a massive humanoid skeleton slumps amid the ruins of the northern one. This skeleton still wears tattered fragments of hide armor, and a large club lies next to one of its bony arms._

**If approaching 1 hour after dusk**:
_A sickly greenish-yellow light flickers inside the second floor of the ruined tower, and the faint sound of eerie moaning can be heard from within._

##### WHAT DO PCs KNOW ABOUT THIS PLACE?

Characters with bardic knowledge or proficiency in History might know a little bit about the keep.

- For characters native to the Devan Vale, Vraath Keep’s story is commonplace (DC 10).
- It’s much more obscure for characters who aren’t from around here (DC 20).
- If Jorr is with the PCs as a guide, he can fill them in on the keep’s story, if the players think to ask him about it.
  - The Keep was famous for guarding the trade route. After the fall of Corvo, the ambitious heir, Amery Vraath, claimed the entire Witchwood and sought to reinforce his claim by "cleansing" it of monsters and threats. He targeted giants who raided caravans from time-to-time, leading a small army to their steading and razing it after a furious battle. Returning home, they feasted, putting giant heads on spikes over the gatehouse.
  - One week later, the surviving Twisted-Tusk giants attacked during a thunderstorm (some say they conjured it). By dawn, four giants lay dead and the keep was empty, survivors being carried off to make a victory feast for the giants. They say the ghost of Amery Vraath still haunts the keep.

#### Interrogating Prisoners

If PCs take prisoners, as long as they believe Koth lives, they have advantage against intimidation. If they are deceived, or magically coerced, they know:

- Koth gathers information on the region in preparation for an assault on Davik's Ferry
  - Day 12, unless delayed. "In X days..."
- The tower ghost isn't real
- The Horde is coming - thousands of warriors and monsters
- Ozyrrandion has visited the Keep three times, speaking only to Koth, carrying messages and reports.

### 1 Ruined Gatehouse

_The keep’s gatehouse has seen better days. The watchtowers to either side have partially collapsed, and several large boulders lie strewn about. The decayed remains of two sets of large wooden gates lie in a heap on the ground._

**DC 14 Survival**: several humanoids, medium and small, as well as dire wolves, and one unidentified large creature.  Easy to approach this point at night, without lights, as everyone is indoors.

### 2 Gardener's Shack

_A rotting, wooden shack sits to the east of the keep itself, near an overgrown patch of weeds that might have once been a fine garden._

**DC 10 Intelligence check**: it's barely standing, and would be dangerous to enter.
Every time a creature enters, roll 1d20 - on a 7 or lower, it collapses. The DC raises +2 for each entry. On failure: **Dexterity Save DC 15** vs 2d6 bludgeoning damage, and the whole keep knows you're here.

### 3 Courtyard

_This courtyard of hard-packed earth has an eerie air of desolation. Jagged boulders embedded in the ground seem to have been dropped here, or thrown from a great distance; many of the walls bear large dents and cracks where these boulders might have once struck. Two massive skeletons lie at opposite ends of the courtyard, one propped up by the watchtower and the other sprawled at the far end by a building that could be a stable. To the south, where a section of the keep’s outer wall has collapsed, a third giant skeleton lies partially buried in the rubble._

### 4 North Tower

_An empty tower with nothing remarkable inside._

If players retreat and return, a goblin wolf rider is stationed here to keep look-out.

### 5 South Tower

_An empty tower with nothing remarkable inside._

If players retreat and return, two orcs are stationed here to keep look-out.

### 6 Wolf Stable

DC 8 Perception check: listening at the door, you here the sound of goblin voices talking low, with an occasional chuckle or grumble.

_This large wooden building has a thin layer of dry, moldering straw strewn across the floor. To the east, an open area contains a decrepit, dust-caked forge and a large mound of moldy straw. A battered table with four chairs sits in the middle of the room. To the west, four horse stalls divide up the remaining space._

2 [dire wolves](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16841-dire-wolf), and two [goblin wolf riders](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195047-goblin-boss) (advantage while mounted).

**Tactics**: surprised goblins flee and whistle for wolves, mount, then try to push out to courtyard. If wolves get to 5hp, the goblins try to flee, surrendering if cornered.

### 7 Manticore Nest

_What this squat building might once have been used for is unclear. All the furniture has been crushed into rubble, and a large nestlike mound of tree branches, bones, bits and pieces of foul-smelling animal parts, and long, thin spikes of some sort of black material fi lls the northwest corner of the room. A gaping hole in the roof in the northeast corner provides a view of the sky above._

A manticore lairs here, using a hole in the roof to enter/exit. New to the group, it climbs up to look at disturbances, but doesn't join unless attacked or under Koth's orders. Not very bright, it might assume strangers work for Koth (DC 10 Persuasion); growling at them to talk to Koth, in the tower.

**Tactics:** perches on roof to hurl spikes, then flies to area 8 and claws at the roof to alert Koth before flying down to melee. If brought to 10hp, tries to flee north to mountains, never to return.

**Treasure:** bribed to join the horde, the manticore's nest is replete with stolen loot.

- 250gp, 50pp
- a gold chalice worth 100gp
- a filthy red silk cloak embroidered with gold thread depicting a rampant lion worth 250gp (if cleaned).

### 8 Barracks

_The interior of this stone building is open and spacious. Wooden timbers support the roof above, and here and there the remains of what were probably once interior dividing walls are evident. Two large boulders sit on the floor to the south, near a large hole that has been knocked in the southeast wall. Rubble from this hole has been piled in a circle to form a crude fi re pit. Against the west wall of the room are arranged four double bunk beds, each strewn with filthy covers. Another bed, this one larger, sits near the south wall, partially obscured by a badly damaged wooden screen. A poorly stuffed owlbear looms in the center of the room._

**Foes:** Karkilan the [minotaur](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16958-minotaur), and four [Orc veterans](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16817-bugbear).
**Tactics:** Surprised, it takes the orcs a round to get up and arm themselves; one tries to make the courtyard and call for help. (If goblins are surprised, 2 rounds to arm and emerge.) Karkilan moves to 10, warning Koth asap and remaining at his side for the battle.

**Treasure:** arms and weapons, plus a sack under Karkilan's bed with a fistful of rubies worth 300gp.

### 9 War Room

_A large table fills this room, barely leaving enough room for the half-dozen chairs arrayed around its edge. Pinned to the table’s surface by daggers is a massive map of the region._

**Tactics:** If under attack, Koth rushes to get the map into his Bag of Holding (5 rounds).

### 10 Warlord Koth's Quarters

_This circular chamber has been outfitted with a mix of furniture, including a large desk, an overstuffed chair, a massive four-poster bed, and a large easel that holds a sizable canvas covered with a sheet. A flight of stairs arcs up along the south wall to the upper floor, and a fair amount of rubble from a hole in the ceiling lies heaped on the base of these stairs._

**SECRET:** unknown to [Koth](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/338584-anchorite-of-talos), there is a secret trapdoor to the Vault. DC 20 Perception to find it.
**Tactics:** Surprised, [[Warlord Koth]] tries to flee up to 11 or out to 8 for reinforcements. If cornered, attempts to flee with his potion of flying until he can safely summon his griffon mount.

**Treasure:** Koth wears a Bag of Holding (which contains a potion of flying, 500gp, ,1500sp, a bronze griffon [figurine](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/5413-figurine-of-wondrous-power) and two greater healing potions)

### 11 Fake Haunt

_The roof of this circular chamber has partially collapsed to the southeast. Massive timbers slump against the floor, fallen from the rafters above. A huge gap in the tower’s east wall allows a brisk breeze into the chamber, which has four windows in the remaining walls. Three large boulders lie on the floor amid a halo of fractures and cracks. Mounted about ten feet from the hole in the wall is what looks like a humanoid figure, lashed to a framework of wooden beams, arms upraised. A glow from beneath it illuminates the figure in a pale light. The reek of rotting flesh fills the room._

The corpse was a farmer the goblins killed and brought back some time ago, lashed to a frame and illuminated by a small brazier with a _continual flame_ spell on it (worth 100g). Late at night, an Orc keeping watch howls to add to the illusion.

### 12 Vraath Vault

The trap door from area 10 opens onto a 5ft wide vertical shaft with a rusty iron ladder, descending 40 feet into darkness.

_The walls of this small vault are of worked stone, the ceiling twelve feet overhead. The air is thick and smells stale and slightly smoky. Three alcoves have been cut into the walls, two to the north and one to the east. Each alcove is sealed by an iron gate and locked with chains and a large padlock. Beyond one alcove are several shelves bearing no less than ten small iron coffers. Beyond the second is a small desk and chair, the desk piled high with papers and books. Beyond the third sits a single large trunk. A human skeleton lies slumped against the eastern alcove’s gate. Still dressed in tattered chainmail and feebly gripping a sparkling bastard sword, the skeleton has a massive arrow protruding from its ribs._

DC 20 History: this is Amery Vraath.
DC 15: pick the locks or break down the rusty iron gates.

**Treasure:** Amery wears Gauntlets of Flaming Fury, and an Amulet of Health.

- ten iron coffers embossed with Vraath's coat of arms, each locked (DC 15)
  - six contain coins totalling: 2,500sp, 2,100gp, and 120pp.
  - three contain irrelevant diplomatic notes
  - the final one contains the deed to Vraath Keep, and could be used to establish a claim to the keep later if desired.
- second alcove is a private study; journals from Amery detailing feud with giants, destroying their village, the victory feast and vile mounting of heads, and an arrogant final note about 'permanently' solving the problem.
  - a trunk with the Keep's greatest treasures: a polished giant's skull on a velvet cushion, a coat of blue dragonscale mail, and a staff of healing.
