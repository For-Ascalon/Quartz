_A wide expanse of dark water has fl ooded the woodland in this low valley. Trees still protrude from the calm, dark waters here and there, but many large reaches seem to be little more than open pools of algae-choked water. The trill of frogs and the whine of insects fills the air. The forest road leads right down to the edge of the flooded section, up to a rickety-looking causeway made of thick planks of wood lashed together with mossy rope. The wooden causeway runs for several hundred feet through the boggy patch, only a foot or so above the water_.

_Up ahead you can make out the wreckage of a wagon, lying on its side and half-sunk in the flooded forest, about thirty feet from the causeway._

**Terrain**:

- wood is slick and uneven; DC 10 Dexterity check vs Prone if you dash.
- 1 action to scramble up from Shallow Bog (difficult terrain)
- Deep bog is 4x movement cost; DC 15 Survival to notice before stepping in it

**Combat**:

- 6-headed [hydra](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195084-hydra) in the water near the wagon; head resting on wreckage.
  - Senses PCs in 30ft automatically, with +6 Perception otherwise.
  - DC 15 Perception to spot its single reptilian head from the causeway
- Reduced to two heads, it flees downstream, deeper into the swamp.

**Treasure**:

- orc, human, and elf skeletons around the wagon, partially devoured
- mithral chain mail
