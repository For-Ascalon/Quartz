### Jorr's Cabin

_The trail leads past a small cabin in a deep forest glade. A ramshackle front porch is littered with fishing baskets and skinning frames. The cabin overlooks a dark bayou or lake, with old gray cedar trees draped in moss rising out of the water. An old skiff is tied up on the shore nearby, and a little smoke curls from the fieldstone chimney_.

Two mastiffs lie under the porch; DC 12 Perception check to notice them. If approached within 60ft, they lunge out barking, stopping 10ft from PCs, snapping furiously. They attack if players move closer or threaten. At the noise, Jorr moves to the window to see what's going on, calling them off if PCs aren't threatening.

[[Jorr Nathovich]] is a [woodsman](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5174957-scout). If a fight breaks out, he shoots from a loophole with 3/4 cover and tries to flee out the back door if overcome.

_The cabin’s door opens, and a lean woodsman of indeterminate age steps onto the porch. He has a seamed, leathery look to his face and arms, and a big curved knife sits in a sheath strapped to one thigh_. “Don’t get many visitors out here,” _he rasps_. “Who are you, and what do you want?”

#### Dialogue

Unfriendly [[Disposition]] if PCs are mean to the dogs, or hostile acting to him; otherwise Indifferent, but moves to Friendly automatically if he learns the PCs intend to fight Orcs.

**We want to stop the goblin raids, and Sorana said you know the forest**.

- “Orcs and goblins! Damn them all. Wood’s infested with them right now. All right, what can I do for you.”

**What have you seen**?

- “Little wolf riders all over, scouting the old trails. Big fighters all along Dawn Way - been keeping my distance.”

**Where’s the goblins’ lair or stronghold**?

- “They live up in the [[Cinderspires]] mostly, but seems like we got a big war party in the forest. Maybe they come down the Old Forest Road, or might be they came down the Dawn Way, over Skull Creek. Probably the Dawn Way - the old road gets very treacherous coming down out of the mountains.”

**Do you know where Vraath Keep is**?

- “Sure - go west to reach the Dawn Way; it’s seven or eight miles north of there. Wouldn’t surprise me if some of those raiders are holed up there. Just the sort of foul place savages would hide.”
  - If Friendly: "Haunted. Restless dead howl the night away up there - best to keep away."

**We want to hire you as a guide**.

- “That would be wise - the forest is dense and dangerous. 5 gold a day, and I'll guide you; would be 10, but since you're killing orcs I'm inclined to be generous.”
