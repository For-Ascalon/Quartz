**Timeline**: late night after PCs return to Davik's Ferry after Skull Creek Bridge.
**Summary**: a town council meeting is called where players get to weigh in on how to proceed.

The players are summoned to Speaker Vistan's home, where Captain Sorana, Delora Davikov, Kallan, and Yormel await. The town council discusses how to react to the horde, and there are three general options:

- **Stand and Fight**: insane. suicide. thousands of well-armed warriors and monsters vs a few dozen militiamen and adventurers. [[Massacre at Davik's Ferry]]
- **Diplomacy**: if they actually try it, use [[Easy Skirmish]], and if they persist use [[Into the Horde]], where Kharn mocks them and offers to release half if the others submit to be sacrificed on the war altar. If combat breaks out, they try to capture the party for sacrifice. Use [[Captured]] encounter for a chance at freedom.
- **Evacuate**: by far the best option. [[11 Next Move]]

\*You are all gathered in the sitting room of the Speaker’s house. Here with you are Kallan, the halfling who owns the Old Bridge inn; Captain Sorana; Delora Davikov, the woman who owns the town’s livery stable; and a tall, sour-faced man named Yormel, who is evidently a person of some property. Along with Norro Vistan, these people comprise the Town Council.

_“Well, you all know why we’re here,” Wiston says without preamble. “In a matter of days we’re going to have an army of orcs, goblins, and monsters on our doorstep. What do we do? Fight? Try to talk to them? Abandon the town and flee? Or do nothing and hope they don’t come this way?”_

#### Council Member Opinions

- **Delora**: Grim. A seasoned ex-adventurer herself, Delora has no illusions about how fighting or talking are likely to work out. She advocates flight, the sooner the better.
  - _“I’ve seen towns put to the sword before. Death or slavery await us if we remain here. We must gather the things we can and flee east, the sooner the better!”_
- **Yormel** (Fight): Sarcastic and snappish. He favors fighting, because he can’t bear the thought of abandoning his property or paying a hefty ransom.
  - _“We can’t let them take everything we’ve worked so hard for, just because we lack the nerve to stand up to these barbaric ruffians!”_
- **Kallan** (Negotiate): Optimistic, even-tempered. Kellin advocates sending emissaries to the hobgoblins and trying to buy them off with whatever it takes.
  - _“They want treasure, don’t they? Let’s give it to them this time, but see to our defenses. If they come back next year and ask for more, we’ll be ready to fight then.”_
- **Sorana** (Evacuate): Also grim. It’s her job to execute whatever decision the council makes, so she does not advance any particular course of action—but if asked, she replies that she thinks it would be suicide to fight.
  - _“It’s not for me to say. I’ll try to do whatever you ask me to, but I can't stop an army of thousands.”_
- **Vistan** (Undecided): Flustered and indecisive. The speaker doesn’t really know what to do. He leans toward trying to talk to the hob goblins, but he doesn’t expect that would work.
  - _“I guess I don’t see that it could hurt to at least ask them what they want . . . maybe we should tell folks to get their children and elderly in wagons heading east, while we still can. What do you think?”_

#### Interaction

If the players argue persuasively, their opinion should carry the day. Otherwise, it's a DC 15 Charisma check to change a council member's mind who holds a strong opposing opinion. That DC is reduced by 2 for each of the following scenarios:

- Players show the council Koth's map and notes.
- Players defeated the [[Goblin Raid]]
- Players defeated the [[Chimera Attack]]
- Players convince Jorr to follow them to Davik's Ferry and testify
