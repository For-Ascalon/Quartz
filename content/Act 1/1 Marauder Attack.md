### Approach

_The afternoon sun beats down on you; the air is hot and still. The sparsely settled lands of the Devan Vale are starting to grow monotonous, with a seemingly endless line of dusty flyspeck towns. The town of [[Davik’s Ferry]] lies a few miles ahead of you. It’s a settlement just on the eaves of the Witchwood, and the best place from which to begin exploring the nearby forest_.

---

Roll one stealth check for the Orcs (+3)

- Players roll Perception to beat the result; Any who fail are _**surprised**_.

---

The road crests a small rise and descends into a dusty grove in a large, shallow dell. An abandoned farmhouse, clearly falling to ruin, can be partially glimpsed through the trees to the right. You’ve passed a dozen spots much like this one already today, but this one feels wrong. Suddenly, there is an explosion of movement and sound as several hulking, howling orcish warriors burst from the trees and rush toward you.

**Failed checks**:  Your horse suddenly rears up, spooked by the attack - make an Animal Handling check, DC 10 to avoid being thrown!

### Foes

**First wave**:

- 6 [[Orc Warrior|orc warriors]], bows as long as possible, then melee

* 2 [[Hellhound|hellhounds]]
* Zarr ([Doom Hand Cleric](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17188-orc-eye-of-gruumsh))
* Uth-Lar [[Orc Champion]] (Bladebearer)
  **Second wave**:
* 6 [[Orc Warrior|orc warriors]], join at end of 3rd round, from south (**off map**)

### Tactics

- If the Bladebearer or Cleric are killed, the warriors flee north to the Witchwood (3 miles away)
- Zarr, Uth-lar, and dire wolves hang back until they realize the party is strong. Uth-lar and wolves target nearest hero, while Zarr blesses and smites.
- Zarr blesses the warriors fighting the players; and does so again when reinforcements arrive.
- If the first wave and at least one dire wolf are slain, Zarr and Uth-Lar retreat to the farmhouse to make their final stand!

### Developments

Zarr, Uth-lar, and the orcs are fanatics; most would prefer to die before divulging any information at all, although a successful charm person spell could get one talking. Key facts the PCs can learn include the following:

- The warband’s base is an old human castle in the forest.
- The marauders serve the Warlord Koth, whom they describe as a mighty sorcerer.
- Koth’s chief lieutenant is Karkilan, “a great demon, as big as an ogre.”
- More warbands are reaving the lands around “the human river-town” preparing for the “Day of Ruin.”
- “You may have won today, stupid humans, but our day is coming! The Red Hand will destroy you all!”

These might also come out as threats and boasting, if someone passes a DC 15 Persuasion/Intimidation check.

### Treasure

In addition to the gear they carry, the marauders found 355 gp on the merchant and her guards. The money pouch is currently sitting beside the bodies (Search DC 10 to find), since Zarr has not yet divided it out. The dead guards are dressed in studded leather armor and carried light crossbows and longswords, none remarkable.
