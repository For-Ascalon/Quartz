**Timeline**: An hour or two after PCs arrive in Davik's Ferry.

*You’re just settling into your rooms when a knock comes at the door. Outside in the hallway stand a tall, balding man of about fifty and a capable-looking swordswoman*. “Sorry to trouble you, travelers, but I’d like a few minutes of your time,” *the man says*. “My name is [[Speaker Norro Vistan|Norro Vistan]] - I’m the Town Speaker for Davik’s Ferry. This is Sorana, captain of the guard. I’ll get right to the point: We’re in a lot of trouble, and I was hoping I could persuade you to help us."

“Our town is under attack,” *the speaker begins*. “Orc and goblin raiders have been harrying our lands for several days now. They’ve attacked and killed people in the outlying homesteads, and they’ve been waylaying travelers along the Dawn Way.”

*Captain Soranna adds*, “We’ve always had some trouble with them - the odd raid on homesteads out west from time to time, unfortunate but manageable - but this is different. It looks like a large, aggressive tribe has moved down out of the Cinderspires. Frankly, I fear they might have the numbers to sack the town.”

“Beyond the immediate threat to our homes, the road is our lifeblood,” *the Speaker continues*. “If the orcs make the Dawn Way impassable to the west, trade won’t come this way, and we’ll be ruined. Not only do we need to repel the raiders from our town, but we’ve got to keep that road open. Can you help us?”

#### Encounter
If players impressed the Sergeant entering town, Speaker Vistan and Sorana are Friendly; otherwise they are Indifferent, view this as strictly a business interaction with sellswords. Vistan is honest and blunt:

**What does it pay**?
- “You’d be helping a lot of people if you could deal with this problem.”

**No really, what does it pay**?
- “We could pay you as much as 100 pieces of gold each, if you can drive off these orcs.”

**Pay us more**.
- “We can’t do better than that.” 
	- If the speaker is friendly: “You drive a hard bargain . . . all right, then, 200 each, and I'll see if I can work out any bargains with townsfolk to help get you equipped.”  (1/2 off)

**What do the orcs want**?
- “I've no idea - our lives, our gold, our lands? They don’t think we’re strong enough to stop them from taking whatever they want, I guess.”

**Large tribe? How many, then**?
- “We’re not sure. A hundred warriors, maybe.”
	- If the speaker is friendly: “There have been at least three different warbands around our lands at the same time, each about fifteen to twenty strong, so fifty at the minimum.”

**Why don’t you take care of it**?
- “We tried. We sent a dozen guardsmen west on the road to find out where the hobgoblins were lairing, but they got cut to pieces a few miles inside the forest.”
	- If friendly: “The orcs are using fearful monsters and evil magic against us. Until you showed up, we haven’t had any heroes here who could stand up to them.”

**Monsters? What kind**?
- “Wolves big as horses, some travelers have even reported manticores!”

**How do we get to the Witchwood**?
- “Cross the river and follow the Dawn Way until it bends north. The road goes all the way through the forest. Or you could strike north from here and take the old Witch Trail, but not many people go that way.”

**Ever hear of Vraath Keep**?
- “Aye, it’s an old ruined castle about 15 miles or so west on the Dawn Way.”
	- If friendly: “Hmmm . . . it’s certainly possible that the orcs have seized it for their stronghold. It’s supposed to be haunted, but they might not care.”

#### Conclusion
Sorana offers this final advice:

“If you’re going to be wandering around in the Witchwood, I would advise you to seek out Jorr. You can’t fi nd someone who knows the woods better. His cabin is out of the way, but it could be worth the walk. If you take the Witch Trail, go left at the first big trail crossing; Jorr’s cabin is about seven miles in. Or, if you’re on the Dawn Way, take a right on a trail about nine miles from the forest edge. Jorr’s cabin is near the river.”

#### The Dawn Way
Cuts through the western portion of the Witchwood for about 40 miles, with rudimentary log shelters built by teamsters in clearings near the road every few miles. Every hour or two you pass an old woodcutter or trapper's cabin, or a long-abandoned homestead. It also frequently crosses bogs and small streams over sturdy dwarf-made stone bridges.

#### The Old Witch Trail
A footpath from the north fields to the Old Forest Road. The Witchwood is crisscrossed by old game trails and paths cut by hunters, trappers, woodcutters, or the ancient elves long ago - best stick to the known trails to avoid getting lost.
