Speaker Vistan and Captain Sorana seek out the players:

*The town of Davik’s Ferry is empty. Houses stare vacantly out at silent roads. The sun has just vanished behind the formidable scar on the horizon that is the Cinderspires. Speaker Vistan takes a deep breath and wipes tears from his eyes with the back of his hand. “It seems we’re about out of time,” he says. “We’d best be going now if we want to keep ahead of the worg riders.” 

*Captain Sorana nods to you. “You’ve already done us good, heroes. Without your warning, we’d have all been asleep in our beds when the horde arrived on our stoops. You’ve saved many lives today. For that you have my thanks. That being said, there’s nothing to stop the horde before they reach the walls of Brindol.”*

*“If there’s anything more you can do to slow down the Red Hand, we’d be grateful,” the Speaker says. “I’ll personally make sure that Duke Jareth knows what you’ve done here.”*

#### Final Q&A

- Will we get paid for slowing down the horde?
	- *“I can’t speak for Duke Jareth, but I’m fairly certain he’ll reward your services generously.”*
- What are your plans now? What of the people of Davik’s Ferry?
	- *“I reckon we’ll head east till we reach Tarleton. We’ll soon see if the horde is following or not. In the worst case, we move on to Simion Gap, and then Kamar. We’ll run all the way to Brindol if we have to. I’ve already sent some riders ahead to spread the news, warn the towns, and alert Lord Jareth to the dire news.”*
- Why Brindol?
	- *“Brindol’s the largest town in the vale. It’s got a good wall, and a standing army. If we can spread the word and raise the country, we might be able to stop the Red Hand there.”*
- There’s a lot of hobgoblins out there. Can Brindol’s army really stop them?
	- *“They’ll have to. If we can’t stand at Brindol, we can’t stand anywhere.”*
- What should we do to help?
	- *“I suppose you could try to stay in front of the horde, keep the worg riders and such from slaughtering the folk of the vale-towns before they can flee. But what worries me most is the reports that the Old North Road and the Corvo Trail are blocked. If we’re going to get reinforcements from outside the vale, they’ll have to come in from the north. I guess I’d say go north and find out if there are any more orcs marching from that direction.”*
- What’s the best way to get to Corvo?
	- *“Well, normally I’d say the Old North Road. But unless you want to run into that blockade, I wouldn’t recommend it. You could take a boat up the Fenwash to Lake Ruin, but you'd be passing by the horde. You can always try to trek cross-country, but the Witchwood is dangerous enough without having to cross the Blackfens on foot after... Your only other option is to head east on the Dawn Way. At Kamar or Brindol, you’ll be able to cross the Deva River and follow the Old North Road until you reach the Corvo Trail. It’s up to you. The river route is probably more dangerous, but a bit faster. If you head east, I can give you several horses. If you head north from here, feel free to take any of the boats moored in town. We won’t need them.”*

### Recap
This is an excellent time to recap what players have learned before moving to Act 2:
- the Red Hand Horde is a massive army bent on conquering the Vale
- they are heading for Brindol, sacking each town along the Dawn Way as they go, with an estimated arrival date of:  Day X
