### Riding into Town: Day 1

_The road descends into a small town built mostly on the near side of a broad, sluggish river. Six old stone piers jut from the water, marking the spot where a bridge once stood, but the span itself is long gone. Instead, a couple of long thick ropes cross the river, each secured to a flat-bottomed ferryboat. Brown fields and green orchards surround the town_.

_A group of armed townsfolk - three in leather, one in mail - stands guard, watching you warily_.
“Halt and state your business, strangers,” _one of the guards says_.

#### NPCs

- Sergeant Henrik ([guard](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16915-guard))
- 3 [militia](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16829-commoner)

#### Tactics

Not interested in fighting; if attacked, blow a horn to warn the town and summon help, withdrawing toward the Toll House.

Henrik is in charge at this watchtower. A burly, gruff man in scale mail, with a curled mustache. Happy to let travelers pass as long as they don't seem like trouble. (Indifferent; DC 10 Persuasion to upgrade to Friendly and open up more.)

(**ODD SPECIES**: Orcs or other odd species in the party cause him to insist they stay put while he fetches Captain Sorana. She arrives with 6 more guards and questions players more thoroughly.)

#### Dialogue

If the characters voice any of the following statements or questions, Hersk responds as indicated.

**We don’t have to tell you anything**!

- “No need to be rude, you! I’ll warn you to mind your manners and stay out of trouble while you’re in Drellin’s Ferry. Pelor knows we’ve got enough of it without thugs and ill-mannered louts looking to start some more.”

**We’re going to explore an old castle in the Witchwood**.

- “Suit yourself.”
  - If friendly, he adds, “But you should be careful, friends—the woods are crawling with orcs and goblins. I wouldn’t advise it.”

**We just fought a bunch of orcs**.

- “Indeed! Where? Bands of those murderous savages have been skulking near the town for days now. You’re lucky you came through it!”

**Where can we stay**?

- “The Old Bridge Inn is just ahead, on your left. Or, if you prefer, try the Green Apple, on the other side of the square. I like the Old Bridge, myself.”

**Can we help you with your troubles**?

- “Ah, I won’t deny that we’re in a hard spot, and you look like capable sorts. Go over to the Old Toll House—that’s the big stone building, there—and ask for Captain Sorana.”
