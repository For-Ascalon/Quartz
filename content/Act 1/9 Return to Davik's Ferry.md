### Ill Tidings
If players don't seek out Vistan or Sorana, they show up within an hour of their return.

*The Town Speaker steps into the inn’s common room. Captain Sorana follows him, along with another tall, broad-shouldered woman with blonde-gray hair—Delora Davikov, the master of the town’s livery stable. The speaker ushers the rest of the townsfolk out of the room; Kallan, the halfling who runs the Old Bridge, helps him. Then the four townsfolk sit down across from you. “Well?” asks Vistan. “What happened? Were you able to run them off?”*

	- *“There are still Twisted-tusks in the wood? We thought they were all long dead!”*

	- *“Good! So you’ve dealt with the hobgoblins, then?”*

	- *“That’s our vale, all right. What do these goblin scribbles say?”*

	- *“But you can stop them, can’t you? I mean, when you say enormous, you mean a hundred or so, right? We could help.”*

	- *“Gods preserve us! We can’t fight an army like that! What are we to do?”*

	- (Wincing) *“Did you have to? When things get back to normal, we’ll need that bridge. There’ll be no trade along the Dawn Way until we build a new one.”*

	- *“I don’t know. Rest for now; I need to talk to the other town elders. I’ll call a council meeting soon and ask you to join us, probably later tonight or first thing tomorrow morning.”*
