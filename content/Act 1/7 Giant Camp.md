### Decrepit Effigy
*The forest grows more ominous as the Dawn Way winds deeper into the woods. You come to a spot where a wide, long-neglected track leads away from the road, heading west. A massive effigy of some kind stands here, a fifteen foot-tall humanoid shape made from a sagging, moss-covered frame. The thing looks almost like a crude giant-sized skeleton. Birds nest in the massive barrel that served the effigy as a head. A wide, flat stone at its base bears large, faded runes.*

At this point on the trail, the Dawn Way cuts through the territory of the Twisted-Tusk tribe (vafinn-tonn thjod - people of the twisted-tusk, after the hunting of mighty boars of the Witchwood).

**DC 13 History**: tribal territory marker, giant.
**DC 16+ History**: hill giants, very old, and treat the Boar as a totemic animal.
If they read Giant: ***vafinn-tonn thjod*** - people of the twisted-tusk.
### Old Karstaag
[[Karstaag]] is an old [hill giant](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/324280-hill-giant-variant) with the **Fling** action:

_**Fling**_. The giant tries to throw a Small or Medium creature within 10 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or be hurled up to 60 feet horizontally in a direction of the giant’s choice and land [prone](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#ProneCondition), taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet it was thrown.

#### Approaching Karstaag
*The old track comes to an abrupt end in the shadow of a fairly large hill that looms up from the surrounding woodlands. At the hill’s base sits the collapsed ruin of what was once a massive and probably quite impressive wooden palisade fortress. Entire trees were used to form the walls of this building, but now the whole thing is little more than a heap of moss-covered, rotting logs. A thin curl of smoke rises up from a great fire pit in the middle of the old walls, where an enormous boar sizzles on a spit the size of a lance. A giant sits by the fire, turning the spit and singing softly to himself in his own language. He is extraordinarily tall and lean, with stooped shoulders, earth-yellow skin marked by angry red blotches, and a wild mane and beard of tangled grey hair. He easily tops ten feet, even while sitting on the ground.*

Karstaag lost his right eye in the battle at Vraath Keep long ago. Now he's old, tired, and lonely, as the rest of his tribe choose to live higher in the mountains now. He lost his father when Amery Vraath attacked their steading, and continues to mourn. He's caught persistent disease from hunting in the bogs, and knows he likely won't live through another winter.

#### Dialogue
Seeing PCs, he slowly stands, clearly in pain, and leans on his greatclub:

*"Damn you blood-sucking vermin, it's not enough you take the whole vale for yourselves, now you come to finish me off?!*

**Tactics:** Old, tired, and sick, Karstaag won't attack unless provoked. Unfriendly, rather than Hostile.

**Diplomacy**:
Begins as Unfriendly. If PCs can shift his [[disposition]] to Friendly, or convince him of the need for his help and heal his wounds, he promises to seek out his surviving kin and ask them to help.
- DC 15 Persuasion check: disposition shifts
- Cure his infection: DC -5, disposition shifts
- Handing over the giant skull from Vraath Keep: DC -5, disposition shifts
- If changed to Friendly, invites them to eat.
	- After achieving Friendly status, another Persuasion check (DC 5) can acquire his help in delaying the Horde, and possibly the Treasure below.
- If combat breaks out, Karstaag fights to the death, no interest in prolonging the inevitable.

**Treasure:** Karstaag has one precious memento of his lost tribe: a tattered [banner](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/5510-banner-of-the-krig-rune) with the giant rune "Krig" (War) emblazoned on it. He might remit this unto the PCs if they return the skull of his father and impress him.
