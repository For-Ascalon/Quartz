# Description
 The major river of the region is the Deva, a broad but slow-moving stream fed by a number of tributaries. For most of its length, the Deva averages 200 to 400 yards in width, reaching depths of 20 to 40 feet in the middle of its channel. The small town of [[Devacross]], about 150 miles upstream of [[Brindol]], is the first place where it can be forded, although a wooden bridge spans the river at Brindol and ferries cross it at [[Kamar]] and [[Davik's Ferry]].
