# Description
 Several volcanic peaks gave this rugged range of hills and low, jagged mountains its name; legends say the plumes of ash and smoke that sometimes rise from the inner hills are caused by a great dragon imprisoned beneath them long ago. The Cinderspires are home to a number of goblin, hobgoblin, and bugbear tribes, now united under the banner of [[Azarr Kul]] - Warchief of the [[Kulkor Zhul]].
