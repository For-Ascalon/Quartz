# Description
Sheltering the Devan Vale from the violent storms of [[Sarmatia]] and the [[Sea of Shadows]], the Carpathian Mountains are a range of steep, mist-shrouded ridges. The lower slopes are thickly forested, home to a variety of dangerous monsters, while the higher slopes are sharply cut by the gorges of many small seasonal streams. A smattering of isolated farms and steadings hidden throughout the range represent the last remnants of the first human settlers to explore the vale - xenphobic and reclusive barbarians who seldom descend to trade with folk along the [[Dawn Way]].
