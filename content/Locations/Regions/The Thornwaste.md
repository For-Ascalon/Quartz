# Description
South of [[The Cinderspire Mountains|the Cinderspires]] lies a vast barren land known as the Thornwaste. A maze of broken hills, briar-choked ravines, and dry, dusty scrubland, these badlands give way to larger and larger stretches of true desert as one goes farther south and west. The Thornwaste has long been the haunt of restless spirits, crazed elementals, and vicious plant monsters. Ancient elven ruins, thousands of years old, still lie hidden in this wasteland.
