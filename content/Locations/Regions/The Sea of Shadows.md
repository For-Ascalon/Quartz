# Description
The Sea of Mists, or the Sea of Shadows, depending on your language and regional dialect, is a vast inland sea that lies east of [[The Carpathian Mountains]]. While its dark waters are heavily traveled by enterprising merchants, it has a sinister reputation due to the vast stretches of orc and goblin nations along its shores.
