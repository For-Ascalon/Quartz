# Description
Far beyond [[The Carpathian Mountains]], a vast, rolling plain stretches north and east for hundreds of miles. This endless steppe is the ancestral homeland of the Orcish people with dozens, or perhaps hundreds, of nomadic tribes following the seasonal rhythms of wild herds, fowl, and weather.
