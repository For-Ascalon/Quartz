## Notable NPCs

---

- [[Captain Sorana]] - guard captain, human woman
- [[Brother Doran]] - priest of Eostra, male human
- [[Sertius the Wise]] - abjuration wizard, male gnome, recluse
- [[Avarthiel]] - Keeper of Eos, druid. Ensures townies respect the forest. Prefers carrot; has stick.
- [[Jarla North]] - pale northerner who moved in a few years ago and bought the general store. She has a SECRET! Oddly excited about the approach of the horde.

## Services

---

**The Green Apple Inn & Tavern**

- _modest_ quality [[inn]], run by an opinionated dwarf named **Tharrma**.
  **The Old Bridge Inn**
- larger, busier [[inn]] of _comfortable_ quality, run by a sweet old kleptomaniac halfling man, [[Kallan Proudfoot]].
  **Jarla's Sundries**
- [[general store]] operated by [[Jarla North]].
  **Partag's Brewhouse**
- used to be a barracks for Dacian soldiers; now a brewery run by a half-orc named [[Partag Grol]].
  **Morlin's Smithy**
- run by [[Morlin Coalheart]], male dwarf [[blacksmith]]. Makes armor and weapons, doing regular smithing in between.
  **Shrine of Eostra**
- two young acolytes, Tomoy and Hesha can perform Medicine checks to treat the ill, or sell up to 3 potions of healing. Brother Doran can ritually cast some low-level spells; treat him as a [[Magewright]] healer.
  **The Ferry**
- 6 minutes, 10ft/round, the boat being 30ft long and 10ft wide; capstans turned by draft horses. 1sp to cross on foot, 3sp with a mount; locals get two free crossings daily. Run by Drathgar, great-grandson of Davik himself.

## Defenses

---

**Town Guard**: 15 regular guards, 9 veterans, and one guard captain

**Militia**: a loose collection of farmers, merchants, tradesmen, and woodsmen (commoners, nobles, and scouts), totaling around 90 people, over half of which are commoners armed with clubs and pitchforks.
