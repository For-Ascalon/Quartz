**Timeline:** between day 4 and 14, if players are slow to head out in search of [[6 Vraath Keep|Vraath Keep]].
**Summary**: A scout patrol attacks the West bank.
**Creatures**: 
- First Wave - 3 [[goblin worg rider|goblin worg riders]], 3 [[worg|worgs]], 1 [[hellhound]] 
- Second Wave - [[Kulkor Zhul War Adept]], 5 [[Orc Warrior]]
	- Arrives 3-5 rounds after engaging the first wave.

Tactics:  the worg riders aren't expecting skilled fighters. They attack randomly at first, but in the second round focus attacks on one PC at a time (especially isolated ones). When the second wave arrives, the War Adept starts with Ice Storm? or Fireball/Lightning Bolt, then conjures a fog cloud or focuses on concentrating spellcasters with Magic Missile.

If badly wounded, the raiders will flee, content in the havoc they have wrought.
