**Timeline:** Between Davik's Ferry and Brindol.
**Summary:** Two ettins and a gang of goblins intercept a shipment of gold.
**Creatures:** Two [ettins](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194990-ettin), five [goblins](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195050-goblin-warrior).

### Introduction

_You spy trouble ahead on the road. In the shade of several tall poplars, a two-horse wagon has been dragged off the road. Small goblins clamber all over the wagon, shouting in wicked glee, while a pair of hulking, two-headed monsters roar and shout at the goblins. You think you can make out a couple of motionless bodies in surcoats of blue and white lying at the side of the road._

The ettins shout warnings in giant and goblin if PCs are spotted. They're content with their spoils, and aren't looking for another fight, but will attack if approached.

**Terrain**: the backroad cuts across dusty farm fields, with little cover. 10ft-wide cart path on a battlemap with a few tree trunks will suffice.

#### Tactics

Goblins aren't stupid. They fire shortbows while goading the ettins to attack, closing to melee only if a weak-looking character gets separated.  If players attack at range, the ettins try to close quickly.

#### Treasure

- 3 locked iron coffers in the wagon. (DC 15 lock) 1,000gp and assorted gems worth another 1,000 in each. 6k total value in all.
- 4 dead guards in Brindol colors. Chain shirts, longswords, longbows.
- a leather satchel near one dead guard: a key to the chests, a sealed letter.
  - Letter addressed to Captain Ervath Ironhelm of the Ironhelm Company, Hammerfist Holds. It's a merc contract signed by the Duke, hiring them for defense of the city. It states upon receipt of payment the Company, almost 200 strong, seasoned dwarves, are to set out immediately.

#### Ethical Dilemma

Players can keep the gold, but then the dwarves will not travel to protect Brindol. If they decide to send it on, they can deliver it personally or give it to a trusted NPC (Vistan, Sorana, a Lionguard) and you can assume it makes it safely.
