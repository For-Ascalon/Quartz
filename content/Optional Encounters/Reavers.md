**Timeline:** shortly after dark, between Davik's Ferry and Brindol
**Summary:** Undead minions are directed to attack the party.
**Creatures:** 1 [wight](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195269-wight), 6 zombies, 2 [specters](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195210-specter), and one [Orc Necromancer](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17295-orc-hand-of-yurtrus)

### Introduction

The Ghostlord has begun sending minions to the Red Hand. This group of scouts include a wight with a handful of zombies and two specters. They answer to an Orc Necromancer who is taking them out for a 'test-drive,' so to speak.

Roll a stealth check (just 1d20 with advantage for darkness). If players set a watch, have them roll against that DC. The following text assumes they fail:

_You’ve spent a hard day riding across the vale. Signs of war are everywhere. The horizon is littered with the twisting smoke-plumes of distant burnings, and most of the farms around here have been abandoned. Bedding down in a sturdy farmhouse, you are just about to seek your bedrolls for the night when a blood-chilling screech rouses you from your rest!_

#### Terrain

Any sketch of a farmhouse, 30x20ft is fine, with at least 2 windows and doors, and a low stone wall outside to take cover behind.

#### Tactics

The wight and necromancer hang back, firing arrows and using support spells, while the zombies and specters do their work. If players attempt to approach the necromancer, the wight engages in melee and fights to the death.

The necromancer tries to flee if he is bloodied, or if the wight is defeated. He starts combat with Bane as soon as he can target multiple PCs, or uses blindness/deafness on rogues and silence on casters.
