**Timeline:** Whenever PCs are in a town evacuating before the Horde arrives.
**Summary:** A succubus disguised as a victim of violence ingratiates itself to the party.
**Creatures:** A [succubus](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4904865-succubus)

### Introduction

_You come upon a sorry scene at the edge of town. Two priests are working to evacuate an infirmary next to a small shrine. Dozens of injured and sick people are clustered around the wagons, including some badly frightened children and others on stretchers who moan in pain and fear. The clerics don’t seem to have much help. One of the clerics, a woman of about fi fty, looks up as you pass by. “Can you spare ten minutes, friends?” she asks. “We need to get these poor people away from here before the orcs arrive!”_

One of the last patients led out of the infirmary is a striking young woman with raven black hair and eyes so dark blue they're almost violet. One priest leads her out, supporting her as she walks - one arm in a sling, and a large bruise on her forehead. Her eyes dart quickly around the busy street before locking onto your group with obvious caution.

- _"Here now, worry not my child. These are the heroes who saved Davik's Ferry, and they're just helping us clear out - such godly folk! My friends, this is Marya - a drunken lout attacked her in the tavern a few nights ago and we've been caring for her."_
- She replies, _"Thank you again for your kindness, brother. I think I can make my own way now."_

She tries to travel with the party to their next destination, if it's not into obvious danger. Otherwise, she asks if they might share a meal with her, as she is low on funds and needs to purchase a horse for her "journey to Brindol". If they share a meal with her, she asks many detailed questions about their adventures and future plans.

- Deception +9, Insight +5, Perception +5 (PP 15)
- If cornered and threatened, she fights, transforming only when Bloodied in an attempt to fly away and escape, reporting what she's learned to Warlord Kharn.  If brought to 15hp, she surrenders to the players' mercy, and tries to get information from them in captivity (or to escape if left alone with one guard).
