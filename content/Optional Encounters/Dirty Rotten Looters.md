**Timeline:** Passing through a town that is being evacuated.
**Summary:** Confront a band of human thugs looting a tavern in the chaos.
**Creatures:** 4 [[Blacktooth Thug]]

### Introduction

Pick a battle map with a town street and fairly large building to represent the tavern.

_Few people remain in this town, but a sudden crash followed by a string of curses draws your attention to what was once a rather high-class tavern, now apparently abandoned and vandalized. As you approach the entrance to the building, four men dressed in leather armor are gathered nearby. Between them is the source of the crash—they’ve dropped a large wine rack on the ground, strewing broken glass all around their feet. They exchange dark looks as they notice the accident has drawn your attention._

_One of the men draws a short sword and says, “Just keep moving, assholes! We’re working this side of the street.”_

_“Easy, Chekov!” one of the others whispers. “I think it’s that group everyone's telling stories about!”_\*

These thugs are part of a criminal gang calling themselves [[The Blackteeth]]. Each carries a small leather pouch of blackroot, which is similar to chewing tobacco and unfortunately discolors its users teeth. This group decided to scavenge what they could from the town, the tavern of which was a favorite place to plan their nefarious capers.

#### Tactics

They are greedy, not brave. They'll fight initially do defend their loot, breaking into two groups and flanking the two closest PCs. If brought below 6hp, they cry out and try to flee; if at least two flee, the last two follow suit.

They leave the broken wine area quickly, to avoid the effect below (difficult terrain).

#### Terrain

The 10ft square where the wine bottles broke acts as [caltrops](https://www.dndbeyond.com/equipment/30-caltrops-bag-of-20) (DC 15 Dex or 1 damage and speed = -10 until regaining 1hp - moving at half speed doesn't need to make save.)

#### Treasure

Bandits have pouches with 50gp worth of coins on their persons.
