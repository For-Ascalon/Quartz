**Timeline:** After Davik's Ferry and before Brindol
**Summary:** PCs meet a gang of orc looters (not everyone's a fanatic in the Horde)
**Creatures:** 1 [[Orc Champion]], 8 [[Orc Warrior]], 1 [[Hellhound]]

### Introduction

*You’re trudging along a road a little before sunset. The temperature hasn’t grown much cooler, and it promises to be a hot and sultry night. You top a small rise, and see a band of orcs hurrying toward you along the road!*

#### Tactics
These orc warriors have longbows instead of javelins.  Half stand firm and shoot, targeting casters, while the others charge in, led by the Champion and Hellhound.

**Morale:** they flee if both Champion and Hound are defeated, or five warriors are slain.
