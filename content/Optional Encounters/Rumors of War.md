**Timeline**: between day 10 and 15 (after the horde crosses Skull Creek, but before reaching DF)
**Summary**: a Lionguard from Brindol delivers news of the Old North Road blockade.

Creatures: Teyani Sura, Lionguard [Scout](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17007-scout).

*A black-haired woman, her face streaked with the dust of the road, trudges into the common room. She wears a soldier’s tunic of blue and white over a shirt of mail, with the stylized image of a golden lion over her heart. She signals the barkeep. “Ale,” she rasps. “And send for your speaker. I’ve got ill news for him.”*
### Information
Teyani is here to warn Speaker Vistan and the town elders about the blockade. Players can either wait to eavesdrop or get the following information with even basic courtesy.
* She is willing to point out where the blockade is on a map.
* Blockade details: four or five ogres, a dozen or so orcs, holed up in a small wooden palisade fort.

*“I ride in the service of the Duke. I was part of a patrol sweeping up the Old North Road. Five days ago we ran into a blockade—orcs and ogres, waylaying any travelers heading into or out of the vale from the north. They were too strong for us, and we had to retreat. My captain sent several of us riding off to warn folks across the vale to avoid the old North Road. So here I am.”*

If players tell her about the Horde, she swears colorfully: 
*"Son of a poxy whore! I'm heading back to Brindol then, that news can't wait. Guess I'll need a fresh horse."* 

If they mention the map, she asks to see it, and if shown, she adds: 
*"The Duke needs to see this! May I take this and deliver it to him?"* 

If refused, she nods and asks them to explain the notations (coded, in orcish, goblin, and draconic). She memorizes it and takes down notes to relay to the Duke.
