# Synopsis

War is gathering in the dry brown hills known as [[The Cinderspire Mountains|the Cinderspires]]. As midsummer settles over the lands of the Devan river valley, smothering everything in heat and dust, the fierce warriors of the Kulkor Zhul—”People of the Dragon” in the hobgoblin tongue—gather beneath the dreaded Red Hand banner, ready to sweep down out of the mountains and conquer the bucolic lands below. Your player characters face an onslaught of fire and steel the likes of which few have ever seen.

# Description

Red Hand of Doom casts the player characters in the role of heroes fighting to stem the advance of Azar Kul’s horde. There are far too many enemy warriors for the PCs to deal with them all personally. Instead, the heroes undertake vital missions that influence the outcome of the war. These missions include the following:

- Spying out the movements of Azarr Kul’s army.
- Defeating roving bands of marauders.
- Leading the effort to rally an elf army to meet the Red Hand horde.
- Disrupting an alliance between the Red Hand and a powerful spellcaster.
- Killing important enemy commanders, champions, and monsters.
- And, finally, infiltrating the Black Temple to defeat the Red Hand war-priests and their leader in their secret stronghold. 

The adventure is divided into five parts, describing five key locations the PCs are likely to explore in the course of the adventure. Event-triggered encounters are also woven into each of the five parts. The adventure locales and events are designed to interact: Events will steer the heroes toward adventure locales, and things the heroes do in the locales will trigger events.

### Player Notes
A few things for you to be aware of before Session 0, where we build our characters and prepare for adventure!

- This is a difficult campaign, designed to be fairly deadly.
- Morale often plays a role in combat, and many intelligent foes might elect to retreat or even surrender rather than fight to the death. It's worth considering how you want to treat those situations as a group.
