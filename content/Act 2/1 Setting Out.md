### Act 2: Setting Out

The players essentially have two choices:

- Go north to Corvo and/or the Blockade (The Blackfens)
- Keep ahead of the Horde and harry its eastward progress (The Elsir War)
  - This is a series of miscellaneous adventures:
    - [[Easy Skirmish]]
    - [[Dirty Rotten Looters]]
    - [[Mercenary Gold]]
    - [[The Not-so-Sick Spy]]
    - [[Reavers]]
    - [[Marked for Death]]

If players head for Corvo, any of the above encounters can be run as they pass through the Vale en route to Brindol or the Thornwastes.  Reavers and Marked for Death work best after the players deal with the Ghostlord.

For notes on timing between adventures, see p. 43.
